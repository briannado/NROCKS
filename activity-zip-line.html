North Fork Valley Canopy Tour

NROCKS’s North Fork Valley Canopy Tour was the first of its kind in this part of West Virginia and remains among the longest and most scenic in the region. It begins with a short practice zip line to shake off first-time jitters while you learn the simple techniques that keep you safely zipping through the trees. After the practice zip, it’s — as they say — all downhill from here (and thankfully so).

With seven zip lines up to 60 feet high and spanning up to 680 feet in length, the Canopy Tour is built to deliver adventurous fun and unforgettable views of the North Fork Valley from high in the trees.

Your trained canopy tour guides handle clipping and unclipping your harness on each cable, so you can let go and enjoy the ride.

Duration

Allow about 2–3 hours for the full tour experience.

Requirements

  • Participants must weigh between 70 and 250 pounds.
  • Typical tour size is 1–8 participants; larger parties should call (540) 437-9901.

What to wear

  • Dress in layers for changing temperatures.
  • Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that does not restrict movement.
  • Short shorts are not recommended because of harnesses; long hair must be tied back.
  • Sturdy athletic shoes or lightweight hiking shoes or boots. No sandals or open-toed shoes (including Chacos or Tevas). No FiveFingers shoes.

What to bring

  • Completed waivers, a small daypack, water, trail snacks, sunscreen
  • Camera (optional — must be secured), rain gear if necessary

Arrival

  • Arrive 20 minutes before your scheduled departure.
  • If you miss orientation, there are no refunds or transfers, and we cannot guarantee a spot on a later tour.
