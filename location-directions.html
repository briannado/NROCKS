Location & Directions

NROCKS Outdoor Adventures is tucked into the mountains near Seneca Rocks.

Address & Contact

NROCKS Outdoor Adventures
141 Nelson Gap Road
Circleville, WV

Email: Contact@NRocks.com
Phone: (540) 437-9901

Important Travel Notes

  • There is no cell service in this area, so do not rely on calling or data as you get closer.
  • GPS may not be accurate unless recently updated.
  • Coordinates: 38°42'2"N, 79°28'20"W

Driving Directions

From Washington, D.C. / Baltimore / Northern Virginia

Locate Interstate 81 in the Shenandoah Valley of Virginia. Once on I-81 South there are two options:

Option 1 (via Rt. 55 West)

  1. Take Exit 296 near Strasburg for Rt. 55 West.
  2. Follow Rt. 55 West through Wardensville, Moorefield, and Petersburg to Seneca Rocks.
  3. At Seneca, bear left at the three-way stop onto Rt. 33 East.
  4. Drive about 11 miles, cross the river at Judy Gap, then turn right onto Rt. 28 South.
  5. Drive about 1/2 mile and turn left at the NROCKS sign and Yellow House Inn onto Nelson Gap Road (gravel).
  6. The NROCKS Welcome Center parking area is just ahead on the left.

Option 2 (via Rt. 33 West)

  1. Take Exit 247B at Harrisonburg for Route 33 West.
  2. Follow Rt. 33 West through Harrisonburg into West Virginia through Brandywine and Franklin.
  3. About 14 miles past Franklin, pass through Judy Gap and take a left onto Rt. 28 South toward Circleville.
  4. Drive about 1/2 mile and turn left at the NROCKS sign and Yellow House Inn onto Nelson Gap Road (gravel).
  5. The NROCKS Welcome Center parking area is just ahead on the left.

From Charleston, WV / Ohio / Pittsburgh

  1. Take Interstate 79 to Weston, WV.
  2. In Weston, take Exit 99 for US Route 33 East.
  3. Follow Rt. 33 East through Buckhannon to Elkins.
  4. At Elkins, continue on 33 East / 33-55 to Seneca Rocks.
  5. At Seneca, bear right at the three-way stop to continue on Rt. 33 East.
  6. Drive about 11 miles, cross the river at Judy Gap, then turn right onto Rt. 28 South.
  7. Drive about 1/2 mile and turn left at the NROCKS sign and Yellow House Inn onto Nelson Gap Road (gravel).
  8. The NROCKS Welcome Center parking area is just ahead on the left.

From Davis, WV / Canaan Valley / Timberline

  1. Take WV Rt. 32 South to Harman, WV.
  2. At the stop sign, proceed onto Rt. 33/55 East for about 12 miles to Seneca Rocks.
  3. At Seneca, bear right at the three-way stop to continue on Rt. 33 East.
  4. Drive about 11 miles, cross the river at Judy Gap, then turn right onto Rt. 28 South.
  5. Drive about 1/2 mile and turn left at the NROCKS sign and Yellow House Inn onto Nelson Gap Road (gravel).
  6. The NROCKS Welcome Center parking area is just ahead on the left.
