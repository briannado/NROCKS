Welcome to the Adventure of a Lifetime!

The Via Ferrata at NROCKS is a one-of-a-kind adventure experience, offering a mile of fixed-anchor, professionally-guided rock climbing. Operating year-round, weather permitting, since the early 2000s, the Via Ferrata attracts thousands of people annually from the East Coast and beyond.

Brave the Premier Via Ferrata in North America

Climb the “Iron Way”, take in the stunning scenery, ascend breathtaking heights, and create memories to last a lifetime — or at least until the next time you visit!

During this 3.5-5 hour long climbing experience, you’ll gain 1085 feet in elevation, cross a 150 foot high suspension bridge over 200 feet long, and reach exposed heights of 280 feet.

Lodging at NROCKS

Having a “home base” to return to after a day of West Virginia outdoor adventure is important, which is why we offer convenient on-site lodging options.

  • Camping tent: Traditional tent sites close to adventure check-in and trail access.
  • Cabin rental: Private cabin stays with added comfort and weather protection.
  • Yellow House Inn: Inn-style lodging for guests who want a classic home-base experience.
  • Guest room: Convenient room-style lodging for short stays and weekend trips.
