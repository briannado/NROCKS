Important notes about driving to the NROCKS Outdoor Adventures

There is NO cell service in this area, so don’t expect to be able to call us (or use your smartphone) as you get closer to us.

GPS units may or may not locate us depending on how recently it has been updated. You may be able to find us as a “place of interest”, or you may need to use the coordinates rather than typing in our address. Our coordinates are: 38°42’2″N – 79°28’20″W

No matter how you plan to find us, print out and bring the written directions with you as a back-up. You’ll thank us later!
