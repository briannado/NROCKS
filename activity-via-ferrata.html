Via Ferrata

The signature NROCKS climbing adventure with fixed anchors and epic views.

Via Ferrata at NROCKS

The Via Ferrata at NROCKS is a one-of-a-kind adventure experience, offering a mile of fixed-anchor, professionally guided rock climbing. All you need for this day on the rocks is some physical ability, an appreciation of the outdoors, and a desire to have a great day outside. The double-fin rocks provide a unique combination of physical challenge and overwhelming beauty.

During this 3.5–5 hour climb you’ll gain 1,085 feet in elevation, cross a 150-foot-high suspension bridge that is over 200 feet long, and reach exposed heights of 280 feet. Operating year-round, weather permitting, since the early 2000s, the Via Ferrata at NROCKS attracts thousands of people annually from the East Coast and beyond.

Initially designed and built to transport troops in Italy during World War I, via ferratas around the world have since become a recreational activity for outdoor enthusiasts. Beginners, experienced climbers, and folks in-between enjoy the Via Ferrata at NROCKS every day.

Under the eye of our experienced guides, you’ll gear up and receive instruction on how to use the two-carabiner lanyard system, which allows climbers to gain elevation while remaining clipped into the safety cable at all times. Then off you go — the higher you climb, the better the views.

Duration

3.5–5 hours, depending on group size and ability.

Difficulty & comfort with heights

The experience can be very demanding or not so demanding depending on fitness. It can also be emotionally taxing depending on comfort with heights. It is not recommended for those working through an intense fear of heights. Review the photos below to gauge whether this climb is a good fit for you.

What to wear

  • Dress in layers so you can adjust as you warm up and cool down.
  • Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that does not restrict movement.
  • Short shorts are not recommended because of harnesses; pants or capris help protect knees.
  • Sturdy athletic shoes or lightweight hiking shoes or boots. No sandals (including Chacos or Tevas) or FiveFingers shoes.

What to bring

  • Completed waivers, a small daypack, about two quarts of water, trail snacks
  • Sunscreen; camera or GoPro (optional — must be secured)
  • Rain gear if needed; fingerless bicycle-style gloves (recommended)

Arrival & group size

  • Plan to arrive 20 minutes before your scheduled departure.
  • Typical group size is 1–14 climbers; larger groups should call (540) 437-9901.
  • If you are late and miss orientation, there are no refunds or transfers, and we cannot guarantee space on a later tour.
