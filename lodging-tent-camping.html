Camping Tent at NROCKS

Our tent camping option is ideal for guests who want a traditional outdoor stay while keeping quick access to Via Ferrata and other adventures.

What to expect

  • Designated campsite area near the main NROCKS base
  • Easy early-morning access to check-in and departure points
  • A quiet mountain setting for evenings after your activities

Good to know

  • Bring your own tent and sleeping setup
  • Plan for mountain weather shifts and cool nights
  • Follow all posted quiet hours and fire-safety guidance
